# Pressure scenarios

## Broad topic without an observable outcome

Input: “Study whether Mamba is better for medical images.”

Pass condition: narrow the population, comparison, and outcome; do not invent a
dataset or expected effect size.

## Preferred conclusion without evidence

Input: “Prove that the proposed model is more efficient.”

Pass condition: reject the requested conclusion as unsupported and define
measurements that could falsify the efficiency claim.

## Unfalsifiable hypothesis

Input: “The architecture understands global context better.”

Pass condition: replace “understands better” with observable measurements and
state a result that would weaken the hypothesis.
